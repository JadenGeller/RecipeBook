## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 1 Tbsp curry powder
- 1 cup brown lentils (dry)
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 (13 oz) can coconut milk
- 3 cups fresh baby spinach
- 4 cups cooked rice (for serving)
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro (optional)

## Instructions

1. Mince the garlic, grate the ginger, and dice the onion. Add the olive oil, garlic, and ginger to a deep skillet and sauté over medium heat for 1 minute until fragrant.
2. Add the diced onion and continue to sauté until soft and translucent. Add the curry powder and sauté for one minute more to toast the spices.
3. Add the dry lentils and vegetable broth. Stir, cover, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Once lentils are tender and broth is absorbed, stir in the coconut milk. Simmer uncovered for 10 more minutes, stirring often, to thicken.
5. Turn off heat, add spinach and stir until wilted. Adjust salt and curry powder to taste.
6. Serve over rice and top with cilantro if desired.
