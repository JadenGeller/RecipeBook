# 5-Ingredient Golden Milk Snack Bites

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/5-ingredient-golden-milk-snack-bites/)*

## Description
Quick and easy, 5-ingredient snack bites with cashew butter, coconut, anti-inflammatory golden milk spices, and naturally sweetened with maple syrup.

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup unsalted cashew butter
- 2-3 Tbsp maple syrup (plus more to taste)
- 2 Tbsp hemp seeds (or substitute other fine seed or plain/vanilla protein powder)
- 1 Tbsp Golden Milk Mix (or individual spices)
- 1 pinch sea salt (optional)
- 1/3 cup coconut flour (or almond flour as substitute)
- 2 Tbsp coconut butter (plus more for frosting)

## Instructions

1. Combine cashew butter, maple syrup, hemp seeds, golden milk mix, and salt in a medium bowl; stir to blend.

2. Add coconut flour gradually until reaching a firm, moldable dough consistency - neither tacky nor crumbly.

3. Adjust flavoring as needed with additional golden milk mix or maple syrup.

4. Optionally swirl in softened coconut butter for a marbled effect.

5. Scoop tablespoon-sized portions and gently roll into balls (yields approximately 12 pieces).

6. Enjoy immediately, roll in shredded coconut, or refrigerate for 10-15 minutes. Optional: top with warmed coconut butter, then chill again to set.

7. Store refrigerated up to one week or freeze for one month.
