## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 3 medium carrots, peeled and chopped
- 3 ribs celery, chopped
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 6 cups vegetable broth
- 2 (19 oz) cans white kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 4 cups kale, finely shredded
- Salt to taste
- Vegan parmesan (optional garnish)

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add carrots, celery, onion, and garlic. Sauté until vegetables begin to soften, about 5 minutes.
2. Add vegetable broth, beans, thyme, oregano, cumin, turmeric, and pepper. Bring to a simmer and cook for 5 minutes.
3. Add kale and stir to wilt. Simmer for another 5 minutes to soften the kale.
4. Add salt to taste if needed. Serve hot with vegan parmesan if desired.
