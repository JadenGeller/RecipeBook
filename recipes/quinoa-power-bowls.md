## Ingredients

- 14-16 oz Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
- 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 Tbsp adobo sauce
- 1 Tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp kosher salt, divided
- 3 cups cubed butternut squash
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 cup dry quinoa
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 2-3 handfuls chopped kale
- Sliced avocado (optional)
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil (for dressing)
- 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 Tbsp honey or maple syrup
- 2 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Toss Brussels sprouts with 1 Tbsp oil, adobo sauce, maple syrup, and 1/2 tsp salt on one baking sheet. Toss butternut squash with remaining oil, paprika, garlic powder, and remaining salt on another sheet.
3. Roast both for 25 minutes, stirring halfway through.
4. Combine quinoa and broth in a saucepan. Bring to boil, reduce heat, cover, and cook 15 minutes until fluffy. Stir in kale to wilt.
5. Whisk together dressing ingredients.
6. Divide quinoa mixture among four bowls, top with roasted vegetables, drizzle with dressing, and garnish with avocado.
