## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 medium red bell pepper, chopped
- 3-4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- Pinch of red pepper flakes
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 1 1/2 cups white rice, uncooked
- 1 1/4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 1/4 cups salsa
- 1 (15 oz) can kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 cup green olives, halved (optional)
- Fresh cilantro or parsley for garnish

## Instructions

1. Soak uncooked rice in water for 10-30 minutes, then drain.
2. Heat oil in a large skillet. Sauté onion and bell pepper for 3 minutes. Add garlic and all spices; cook 1 minute more.
3. Add rice, salsa, and vegetable broth. Bring to a boil.
4. Cover and simmer on lowest heat for 15-20 minutes without stirring or uncovering.
5. Turn off heat, adjust seasonings, then stir in beans and optional olives. Garnish with fresh herbs.
