# 1-Bowl Banana Chocolate Pecan Muffins (Vegan + GF)

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/banana-chocolate-pecan-muffins-vegan-gf/)*

## Description
Fluffy, 1-bowl vegan + gluten-free banana muffins generously studded with pecans and chocolate chips. Perfect for a lazy weekend breakfast or take-along snack!

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp flaxseed meal
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 medium ripe bananas
- 1/2 cup maple syrup
- 1/4 cup avocado oil, melted coconut oil, or vegan butter
- 1 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1 cup gluten-free flour blend
- 1 cup almond flour
- 2/3 cup raw pecans, chopped
- 2/3 cup dark chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease a muffin tin or add paper liners.

2. Mix flaxseed meal and water; let sit 5 minutes to thicken (flax eggs).

3. Add bananas and mash until mostly smooth.

4. Add maple syrup, oil, baking soda, vanilla, and sea salt. Whisk 30 seconds.

5. Stir in gluten-free flour and almond flour until just combined. Fold in pecans and chocolate chips.

6. Divide batter among muffin tins, filling almost to tops.

7. Bake 25-28 minutes until golden brown and a toothpick comes out clean.

8. Cool 5 minutes in tin, then transfer to cooling rack.

9. Store in covered container at room temperature (3-4 days), refrigerator (1 week), or freezer (up to 1 month).

**Yield:** 10 muffins | **Prep Time:** 15 minutes | **Cook Time:** 25 minutes
