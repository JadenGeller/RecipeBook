# Lemony Quinoa Kale Salad with Crispy Chickpeas

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/lemony-quinoa-kale-salad-with-chickpeas/)*

## Description
A hearty salad combining quinoa, massaged kale, and roasted chickpeas with a tangy lemon dressing, ready in about 30 minutes.

## Ingredients

### For the Salad
- 1 1/2 cups cooked quinoa
- 4 cups chopped kale
- 1/4 cup sunflower seeds (roasted unsalted)
- 1/4 cup goat cheese crumbles
- 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 1/2 Tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
- 1/4 tsp each sea salt and black pepper
- 2 cups day-old sturdy bread, torn into 1-inch pieces
- 1 tsp avocado oil or olive oil
- 1 pinch each sea salt and black pepper
- 1/4 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes

### For the Dressing
- 2-3 Tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1-1 1/2 Tbsp maple syrup
- 1/2 tsp Dijon mustard
- Pinch each sea salt and black pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment. Prepare quinoa if needed (combine 1/2 cup dry quinoa with 1 cup water, bring to boil, simmer covered 15-18 minutes).

2. Toss drained chickpeas with oil, salt, and pepper on baking sheet.

3. Bake chickpeas 15-20 minutes until crispy. Add bread pieces at the 8-minute mark, tossing with oil and seasonings; bake bread 7-10 minutes until crusty.

4. Whisk together lemon juice, oil, maple syrup, mustard, salt, and pepper. Add kale and massage for several minutes.

5. Add quinoa, sun-dried tomatoes, sunflower seeds, and cheese. Toss to combine.

6. Once cooled slightly, add roasted chickpeas and croutons. Serve immediately.

**Storage:** Refrigerate leftovers up to 2 days; store chickpeas and croutons separately to maintain crispness.
