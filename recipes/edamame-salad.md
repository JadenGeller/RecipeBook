## Ingredients

- 16 oz frozen shelled edamame
- 1 cup shredded red cabbage
- 1 cup shredded carrots
- 1/2 cup chopped green onions
- 2 cups chopped kale
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro (optional)
- 1/2 cup chopped peanuts or cashews
- 6 Tbsp creamy peanut butter
- 3 Tbsp seasoned rice vinegar
- 3 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 3 Tbsp pure maple syrup
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1/4 tsp ground ginger
- 1 tsp Sriracha hot sauce (or to taste)

## Instructions

1. Cook or thaw edamame per package directions; rinse under cold water.
2. Combine edamame with cabbage, carrots, green onions, kale, and cilantro in a large bowl.
3. Whisk together peanut butter, rice vinegar, soy sauce, maple syrup, sesame oil, ginger, and Sriracha until smooth. Add warm water if too thick.
4. Pour sauce over salad and toss thoroughly.
5. Top with additional nuts if desired. Refrigerate at least one hour before serving.
