# Raw Carrot Cake with Vegan Cream Cheese Frosting

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/raw-carrot-cake-with-vegan-cream-cheese-frosting/)*

## Description
Raw vegan carrot cake made with 10 wholesome ingredients! Tender, 30 minutes to make, and topped with an optional cashew cream cheese frosting!

## Ingredients

### For the Cake
- 2 cups finely shredded carrots
- 2 cups packed pitted medjool dates
- 2 1/2 cups raw walnuts
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/4 tsp sea salt
- 1 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- 3/4 tsp ground ginger
- 1 pinch nutmeg
- 1/2 cup coconut flour
- 1/4 cup raisins

### For the Frosting (Optional)
- 1 1/4 cup raw cashews
- 1/2 cup coconut cream or full-fat coconut milk
- 2 Tbsp lemon juice
- 3 Tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. **Prepare frosting (if making):** Soak cashews in hot water 30 minutes to 1 hour. Rinse, drain, and blend with coconut cream, lemon juice, maple syrup, and vanilla until creamy. Refrigerate.

2. **Prepare carrots:** Grate carrots using a box grater or food processor attachment.

3. **Process dates:** Blend pitted dates until small bits remain or a ball forms. Set aside.

4. **Combine dry ingredients:** Add walnuts, vanilla, salt, and spices to food processor and blend until semi-fine meal forms.

5. **Combine all cake ingredients:** Add dates and shredded carrots back in; pulse gently until loose dough forms. Transfer to mixing bowl, add coconut flour and raisins, stir gently to combine.

6. **Assemble cake:** Line a 7-9 inch springform pan with parchment paper. Press cake mixture evenly into pan using a glass or flat object.

7. **Finish:** Remove cake from pan or add frosting, tap out air bubbles, and freeze 3-4 hours until semi-firm.

8. **Serve:** Let thaw 30 minutes before cutting with a hot knife. Garnish as desired.

9. **Storage:** Keep covered in refrigerator up to 1 week or freeze up to 1 month. Best enjoyed chilled.
