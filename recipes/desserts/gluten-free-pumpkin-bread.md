# The BEST Gluten-Free Pumpkin Bread (1 Bowl!)

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/best-gluten-free-pumpkin-bread/)*

## Description
The ULTIMATE gluten-free pumpkin bread: FLUFFY, tender, spice-filled, and so classic! Dairy-free, simple methods, and just 1 bowl required for this nostalgic fall dessert.

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup avocado oil
- 1 1/4 cup organic cane sugar
- 3 large eggs (pasture raised, organic when possible)
- 1 (15 oz) can pumpkin puree
- 2/3 cup almond flour
- 1 1/3 cup MB 1:1 GF Blend
- 1 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice

## Instructions

1. Position oven rack in the center, preheat to 325°F, and line a loaf pan with parchment paper, leaving excess around the sides for easy removal.

2. In a large mixing bowl, combine oil, sugar, and eggs; whisk until silky. Add pumpkin puree and mix thoroughly. Then incorporate dry ingredients (almond flour, gluten-free flour blend, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, and pumpkin pie spice) and whisk until smooth.

3. Transfer batter to prepared loaf pan and bake for 75-90 minutes until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out completely clean. The outside darkens, but avoid burning (cover with foil if needed).

4. Cool in the pan for ~10 minutes, then use parchment edges to lift onto a cooling rack. Allow 2-3 hours for complete cooling before slicing.

5. Enjoy! Store at room temperature for 2-3 days, refrigerate up to 1 week, or freeze for up to 1 month. Reheats well in an oven or toaster oven.

**Yield:** 12 slices | **Prep Time:** 10 minutes | **Cook Time:** 85 minutes
