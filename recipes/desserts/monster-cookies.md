# Easy Gluten-Free Monster Cookies

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/easy-gluten-free-monster-cookies/)*

## Description
Chewy, tender, peanut buttery, incredibly delicious Monster Cookies that are vegan, gluten-free, flourless, and made in one bowl in approximately 20 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup natural creamy peanut butter
- 2/3 cup maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp sea salt
- 2 cups rolled oats (certified gluten-free as needed)
- 1/2 cup natural colored candy-coated chocolate
- 1/2 cup mini semi-sweet chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. **Prepare**: Preheat oven to 350°F (176°C). Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.

2. **Mix dry base**: In a large bowl, combine peanut butter, maple syrup, vanilla, baking powder, baking soda, and sea salt. Stir thoroughly.

3. **Add mix-ins**: Add rolled oats, candy-coated chocolate, and mini chocolate chips. Stir until evenly distributed.

4. **Shape**: Scoop approximately 1 1/2 tablespoons of dough per cookie onto baking sheets. Flatten slightly with your palm. Top each with extra candies.

5. **Bake**: Bake 11-13 minutes until lightly golden. Cool slightly before serving.

6. **Storage**: Store cooled cookies in an airtight container for 2-3 days. Dough can be frozen and baked an extra 1-2 minutes from frozen.

**Yield**: 24 cookies
