# Perfect Vegan Gluten-Free Chocolate Chip Cookies

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/perfect-vegan-gluten-free-chocolate-chip-cookies/)*

## Description
Perfect vegan chocolate chip cookies made with simple methods and 9 ingredients. Tender, sweet, studded with chocolate, and undetectably gluten-free.

## Ingredients
- 1 1/4 cup almond flour
- 1/4 cup vegan dark chocolate chips
- 1/3 cup gluten-free flour blend
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp sea salt
- 1/3 cup organic brown sugar
- 1/4 cup aquafaba
- 3 Tbsp coconut oil, melted
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Mix almond flour, chocolate chips, gluten-free flour, baking powder, salt, and brown sugar in a large bowl.

2. In a separate bowl, beat aquafaba with a handheld mixer until light and fluffy peaks form.

3. Add melted coconut oil and vanilla to the aquafaba, then combine with dry ingredients until just mixed.

4. Chill dough for 30 minutes. Preheat oven to 375°F and line baking sheet with parchment paper.

5. Scoop 1 1/2-tablespoon portions onto the sheet and gently flatten. Makes about 11 cookies.

6. Bake 12-15 minutes until edges are golden brown.

7. Cool on pan for 5 minutes, then transfer. Store in sealed container up to 4 days or freeze up to 1 month.

**Yield:** 11 cookies | **Total Time:** 57 minutes
