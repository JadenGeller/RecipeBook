# 30-Minute Tempeh Stir-Fry

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/simple-vegetable-tempeh-stir-fry/)*

## Description
A hearty tempeh stir fry with bold flavors ready in 30 minutes, designed for weeknight dinners using leftover vegetables.

## Ingredients
- 3-4 tsp chili garlic sauce
- 4 Tbsp creamy peanut butter
- 4 tsp sesame oil (plus 4 tsp more for cooking)
- 1/3 cup coconut aminos
- 4 tsp lime juice
- 3 tsp freshly grated ginger
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- 3-4 Tbsp water
- 8 oz tempeh, chopped into bite-size pieces
- 2/3 cup chopped green onion
- 4 cups mixed vegetables (bell pepper, broccoli, carrots, kale)
- 3 cups cooked quinoa, brown rice, or cauliflower rice

## Instructions

1. **Prepare glaze**: Combine chili garlic sauce, peanut butter, sesame oil, coconut aminos, lime juice, ginger, and maple syrup in a bowl and whisk together.

2. **Adjust consistency**: Add water until sauce reaches pourable consistency. Taste and adjust seasonings for heat, sweetness, acidity, or saltiness as desired.

3. **Marinate tempeh**: Coat tempeh pieces in glaze, cover, and refrigerate 15 minutes (or up to 48 hours). Prep remaining vegetables during this time.

4. **Brown tempeh**: Heat cast iron skillet over medium heat. Saute tempeh 3-4 minutes per side until browned. Set aside, reserving glaze.

5. **Cook vegetables**: Add sesame oil, green onion, and mixed vegetables to skillet with coconut aminos. Cover and cook 4-5 minutes, stirring occasionally until tender.

6. **Combine**: Add grains and greens, cooking 3-4 minutes. Return tempeh with remaining glaze and cook 1-2 minutes until heated through.

7. **Store**: Refrigerate leftovers up to 4-5 days, or freeze up to 1 month.
