# Garlicky Cherry Tomato Pasta with White Beans

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/cherry-tomato-white-bean-pasta/)*

## Description
A quick, plant-based weeknight dish featuring sauteed garlic and shallot with burst cherry tomatoes, kale, kalamata olives, and white beans. Ready in approximately 25 minutes and suitable for vegan and gluten-free diets.

## Ingredients
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 5 medium garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 small shallot, minced
- 4 cups cherry tomatoes
- 1/2 cup water (or white wine)
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1/2-3/4 teaspoon sea salt
- 1 (12 oz.) pasta package
- 1/2 bunch kale, stems removed, leaves chopped
- 1 (15 oz.) can white beans, drained
- 1/2 cup chopped kalamata olives
- 1/2-3/4 cup reserved pasta water
- Vegan parmesan cheese (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Prepare pasta water:** Bring a large pot of water to boil.

2. **Build the sauce:** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add garlic and shallot; saute until translucent. Add tomatoes, water/wine, red pepper flakes, and salt. Cover and cook 5 minutes until tomato skins begin bursting.

3. **Cook pasta:** Follow package directions; reserve 3/4 cup pasta water before draining.

4. **Combine:** Remove sauce lid; add kale, white beans, and olives. Saute 5 minutes until liquid reduces. Add 1/2 cup pasta water and cook 1-2 minutes. Add cooked pasta to sauce; adjust consistency with remaining pasta water as needed. Taste and season to preference.

5. **Serve:** Top with vegan parmesan if desired. Refrigerate leftovers up to 4-5 days; reheat gently. Not freezer-friendly.
