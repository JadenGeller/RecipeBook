# 1-Pot Golden Curry Lentil Soup

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/1-pot-golden-curry-lentil-soup/)*

## Description
A comforting, one-pot curried lentil soup that's rich with flavor, fiber, and protein and can be prepared in about 30 minutes. The dish is vegan, gluten-free, and features warm spices with coconut milk.

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup water (or oil)
- 1/4 cup thinly sliced shallot or onion
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 Tbsp ginger, minced
- 1 small serrano pepper (seeds removed, minced)
- 2 cups thinly sliced carrots
- Sea salt
- 3 cups vegetable broth
- 3/4 cup canned light coconut milk
- 1 cup uncooked rinsed red or golden lentils
- 2 Tbsp coconut aminos
- 1 Tbsp curry powder
- Fresh lemon or lime juice
- Cilantro (for garnish)
- Coconut cream (optional)

## Instructions

1. Saute shallot in water/oil over medium heat for 3 minutes until softened.

2. Add garlic, ginger, serrano pepper, carrots, and salt; cook 1-2 minutes.

3. Add broth and coconut milk; bring to low boil, then add lentils.

4. Reduce heat to low simmer; add coconut aminos and curry powder.

5. Simmer uncovered 12-16 minutes until carrots and lentils are tender.

6. Adjust seasoning as needed; serve garnished with cilantro and citrus.
