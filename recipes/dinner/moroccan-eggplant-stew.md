# Moroccan-Spiced Eggplant and Tomato Stew

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/moroccan-spiced-eggplant-and-tomato-stew/)*

## Description
A flavorful, satisfying 10-ingredient meal inspired by zaalouk (a warm salad of cooked eggplant and tomato) featuring roasted eggplant, smoky spices, and chickpeas.

## Ingredients
- 1 large eggplant (~1.2 lb), unpeeled and cubed
- 2 Tbsp olive or melted coconut oil (for roasting)
- 1/2 tsp sea salt (for roasting)
- 2 Tbsp olive or coconut oil (for sauteing)
- 1 large white or yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 Tbsp ground cumin
- 1 Tbsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp sea salt (plus more to taste)
- 2 cans (14.5 oz each) diced fire-roasted tomatoes
- 1/2 cup water or vegetable broth
- 1 cup cooked chickpeas, rinsed and drained (optional)
- 1 Tbsp maple syrup or coconut sugar
- 2 Tbsp harissa paste

## Instructions

1. **Prepare eggplant:** Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss cubed eggplant with oil and salt; roast for 30-35 minutes, stirring halfway through.

2. **Saute aromatics:** Heat oil in large pan over medium heat. Cook onions 4-5 minutes until soft, then add garlic, cumin, and paprika for 1 minute.

3. **Build stew:** Add tomatoes with juices and water/broth. Cover and simmer 4 minutes.

4. **Add remaining ingredients:** Stir in chickpeas, maple syrup, and harissa paste.

5. **Combine:** Add roasted eggplant. Cover and simmer 10 minutes on medium-low heat.

6. **Adjust seasonings:** Taste and balance heat, smokiness, and salt as desired.

7. **Serve:** Plate with rice, grain, pasta, or roasted vegetables. Top with fresh lemon, cilantro, or parsley. Add extra harissa if desired.

**Storage:** Refrigerate cooled leftovers up to 5 days or freeze up to 1 month. Reheat on stovetop with additional liquid as needed.
