# Caramelized Shiitake Mushroom Risotto

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/caramelized-shiitake-mushroom-risotto/)*

## Description
Easy-to-make vegan risotto infused with sauteed shallot and caramelized shiitake mushrooms! Hearty, flavorful, and incredibly satisfying.

## Ingredients
- 3 1/2 - 4 cups vegetable broth
- 2 Tbsp avocado or olive oil
- 3/4 cup thinly sliced shallot
- 1/4 tsp each sea salt and black pepper
- 2 cups sliced shiitake mushrooms
- 1 Tbsp coconut aminos
- 1 Tbsp fresh thyme (or dried)
- 1 cup arborio rice
- 1/4 cup dry white wine
- 1/4 cup vegan parmesan cheese
- Fresh chopped parsley
- Additional vegan parmesan for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat vegetable broth in a saucepan over medium heat, then reduce to low to maintain warmth.

2. In a large pan, heat oil with shallot over medium heat; season with salt and pepper. Saute 3-4 minutes, stirring frequently. Add mushrooms and coconut aminos; continue cooking until golden and caramelized. Optionally reserve some mushrooms for garnish.

3. Add thyme and arborio rice; cook 1 minute while stirring. Pour in white wine and cook about 2 minutes until mostly absorbed.

4. Using a ladle, add warmed broth 1/2 cup at a time while stirring constantly. Maintain a gentle simmer throughout the 15-20 minute cooking process until rice reaches al dente.

5. Remove from heat; stir in vegan parmesan. Taste and adjust seasonings as desired; add more broth if too dry.

6. Divide into bowls and top with reserved mushrooms, additional cheese, and fresh parsley.

**Storage:** Refrigerate up to 4-5 days or freeze up to 1 month; reheat on stovetop with additional warmed broth.
