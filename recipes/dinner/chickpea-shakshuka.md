# 1-Pot Chickpea Shakshuka

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/1-pot-chickpea-shakshuka/)*

## Description
A savory, flavorful plant-based dish prepared in a single pot within 30 minutes. This vegan adaptation of the traditional North African/Middle Eastern dish substitutes chickpeas for eggs, making it suitable for breakfast, lunch, or dinner.

## Ingredients
- 1 Tbsp olive or avocado oil
- 1/2 cup diced white onion or shallot
- 1/2 medium red bell pepper, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
- 1-3 Tbsp tomato paste
- 2-3 tsp coconut sugar or maple syrup
- Sea salt to taste
- 2 tsp smoked or sweet paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 pinch cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 pinch each cardamom and coriander (optional)
- 1 1/2 (15-ounce) cans cooked chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- 4-5 kalamata or green olives, pitted and halved (optional)
- Lemon wedges, bread, fresh parsley or cilantro for serving

## Instructions

1. **Saute aromatics**: Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onion, bell pepper, and garlic; saute 4-5 minutes until soft and fragrant.

2. **Add tomatoes and spices**: Combine crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, sweetener, salt, paprika, cumin, chili powder, cinnamon, and optional spices.

3. **Simmer base**: Bring to a simmer over medium heat for 2-3 minutes, stirring frequently.

4. **Cook with chickpeas**: Add chickpeas and olives; stir. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer 15-20 minutes, allowing flavors to develop.

5. **Adjust seasonings**: Taste and modify spice levels as desired.

6. **Serve**: Plate as-is or alongside bread, pasta, or rice. Garnish with lemon juice, olives, cilantro, or parsley.

7. **Storage**: Refrigerate leftovers up to 4 days or freeze up to 1 month. Reheat on stovetop until warmed through.
