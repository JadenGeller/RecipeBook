# Creamy Vegan White Bean Chili

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/creamy-vegan-white-bean-chili/)*

## Description
Creamy, comforting vegan white bean chili with green chiles, corn, and leafy greens. A 30-minute meal perfect for meal prep or weeknights!

## Ingredients
- 2 cups water
- 2/3 cup raw cashews
- 2 Tbsp olive oil (or avocado oil)
- 1 cup diced white or yellow onion
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 3/4-1 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 pinch cayenne
- 2 (4-oz.) cans diced green chiles
- 3 (15-oz.) cans white beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 2 cups baby spinach, baby kale, or chopped kale

## Instructions

1. Blend water and cashews on high until smooth (~1-2 minutes) to create cashew milk.

2. Heat a large pot over medium heat. Add olive oil, onions, and garlic; cook until softened, about 5 minutes.

3. Add cumin, sea salt, black pepper, and cayenne. Cook 1 more minute until fragrant.

4. Add green chiles, drained beans, and corn. Stir to combine.

5. Pour in cashew milk and bring to a light boil. Lower heat and simmer 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.

6. Taste and adjust seasonings. Remove from heat and stir in greens until wilted.

7. Serve warm with cornbread, cilantro, green onions, and hot sauce as desired.

**Yield:** 6 servings | **Time:** 30 minutes
