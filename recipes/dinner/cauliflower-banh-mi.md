# Cauliflower Vegan Banh Mi Sandwich

*Source: [Minimalist Baker](https://minimalistbaker.com/cauliflower-banh-mi/)*

## Description
A plant-based Vietnamese-inspired sandwich featuring savory, caramelized cauliflower, sriracha mayo, and quick-pickled vegetables ready in approximately 30 minutes.

## Ingredients

### For Pickled Vegetables
- 2/3 cup rice wine vinegar
- 1/3 cup hot water
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1 Tbsp organic cane sugar
- 2/3 cup shredded carrot
- 1/3 cup shredded daikon radish

### For Cauliflower
- 2 Tbsp chili garlic sauce
- 5 Tbsp coconut aminos
- 1 Tbsp maple syrup
- 1 Tbsp lime juice
- 1 Tbsp sesame or avocado oil
- 4 cups cauliflower florets

### For Assembly
- 1/2 cup vegan mayo
- 2 tsp maple syrup
- 1 Tbsp sriracha
- 2 small baguettes
- Fresh cilantro, cucumber, jalapeno (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 450°F.

2. Combine vinegar, hot water, salt, and sugar in a jar; stir to dissolve. Add carrot and radish, refrigerate until ready to serve.

3. Mix chili garlic sauce, coconut aminos, maple syrup, lime juice, and oil in a bowl. Toss cauliflower to coat.

4. Heat an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Saute cauliflower until lightly browned, then add remaining marinade and transfer to oven for about 15 minutes.

5. Prepare aioli by combining vegan mayo, maple syrup, and sriracha; adjust seasonings as desired.

6. Toast baguette halves, spread aioli on both sides, add cauliflower with extra marinade, then layer with pickled vegetables and optional fresh ingredients.

7. Serve fresh; store components separately in refrigerator for up to several days.
